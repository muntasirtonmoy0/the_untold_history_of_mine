Chapter 04 // Convergence

Part IV: The Reunion at Noapra (2026)

In 2026, my mother and I traveled back to our old region, staying at my maternal aunt’s home in Noapra, Jashore, with a brief checkup trip to Khulna City Medical College and Hospital.

During our stay, Shaolin and her family visited us at my aunt’s home. As we all sat together in the living room, my aunt watched Shaolin intently. When they left, my aunt and sisters turned to my mother in unanimous approval:

"She is so graceful, calm, and composed. She matches Tonmoy perfectly. They look as if they were made for each other."

Shortly after, a grand gathering of our old school circle brought us all together again. I watched her across the room—her poise, her quiet dignity, her unmatched respectfulness.

I never wanted a temporary girlfriend. If I wanted casual romance, I could have said yes to dozens of proposals over the years. I wanted to marry her. Listening to my family’s constant validation, I made the decision to finally speak.
