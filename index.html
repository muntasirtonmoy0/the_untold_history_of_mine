ACT 04 THE SURRENDER & THE FRACTURE

I never wanted casual romance. I could have said yes to dozens of proposals. I wanted to marry her. Listening to my family’s unanimous validation at Noapra, I made the decision to break every wall I had ever built.

I contacted her closest friend. When asked if I truly loved her, I discarded my pride: "Yes. I love her."

Then the strike landed: Shaolin was deeply in love with someone else—a senior named Roktim.

CRITICAL MOMENT: STRIPPING THE EGO

ACT 05 THE NUMB REALITY & THE FINAL CODE

I—the boy who would cut off lifelong friends until they begged for forgiveness—begged her. And her unwavering, polite loyalty to Roktim was the exact wall that shut me out forever.

The machinery broke. The science focus and intricate lines of code slipped through my fingers. But the ego died with it: insults, disrespect, and words behind my back now mean nothing. The pain neutralized into pure numbness.

Friends tell me: "Tonmoy, you are 18. You're brilliant, you have a massive future, you will find someone better." They don't understand. If I wanted options, I would have taken them. I never wanted an upgrade. I only ever wanted Shaolin.
